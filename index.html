Under the hood

It learns what corn
looks like.

Every map is different: painted signs, drone shots, sun and shadow. So MaizeKit reads each one on its own terms.

  1. Learn the corn. Most of a maze is corn, so its typical colour is the corn.
  2. Find the paths. Anything clearly different, lighter or darker, is path.
  3. Drop the noise. Gaps too narrow to walk, like the space between rows, are ignored.
  4. Route down the middle. Every route keeps to the centre of the path.
